⚙️ Consensus Algorithm Justification
Proof of Authority (PoA) is chosen for this campus-level permissioned environment because:
  • Known validators — University servers act as trusted authorities; no need for mining or staking.
  • Fast finality — Transactions confirm in milliseconds, critical for real-time status updates.
  • Low energy — No computational waste; suitable for an academic institution's infrastructure.
  • Ganache compatibility — Ganache simulates PoA by default, perfect for development and testing.
  • Permissioned access — Only admin-registered wallets can write to the ledger, matching the role-based model.
